Crypto Trading Glossary for Beginners

Plain-language meanings of the terms beginners meet most often, from leverage to stop-loss.

By Dilshad · 4 November 2026 · 4 min read

Reference

Plain-language meanings for common trading terms

Crypto trading has its own vocabulary, and unfamiliar words make it easy to feel lost or to be misled. This glossary is written by Dilshad, an independent cryptocurrency trader based in Trivandrum, Kerala, India, to explain the terms beginners meet most often, in plain language and in alphabetical order.

A to C

Altcoin. Any cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin.

Bear market. A long period in which prices fall and pessimism is widespread.

Bull market. A long period in which prices rise and optimism is widespread.

Candlestick. A chart symbol showing the opening, closing, highest and lowest price for a chosen period.

Cold wallet. A wallet kept offline, such as a hardware device, which is harder for hackers to reach than a wallet connected to the internet.

D to L

Drawdown. The fall from a peak value of your account to its later low point.

DYOR. "Do your own research." A reminder to verify claims yourself before acting.

Exchange. A platform where people buy, sell and trade cryptocurrencies.

Hot wallet. A wallet connected to the internet, which is convenient but more exposed to attacks.

Leverage. Borrowing to trade with a larger position than your own money allows. It magnifies both gains and losses.

Limit order. An order to buy or sell only at a price you choose or better.

Liquidation. The forced closing of a leveraged position when losses use up the money backing it.

Liquidity. How easily an asset can be bought or sold without moving its price much.

M to R

Market capitalisation. The price of one coin multiplied by the number of coins in circulation, used to compare the size of projects.

Market order. An order to buy or sell immediately at the best available price.

Position size. The amount of money placed in one trade. See position sizing explained.

Risk-reward ratio. The potential gain of a trade compared with the amount you are willing to lose on it.

S to W

Seed phrase. The list of words that gives full control of a wallet. Never share it with anyone. See how to avoid crypto scams.

Slippage. The difference between the price you expected and the price at which the order was actually filled.

Spot trading. Buying or selling the actual asset for immediate delivery, without borrowing.

Stablecoin. A cryptocurrency designed to keep a steady value, often linked to a currency such as the US dollar.

Stop-loss. An order or rule that closes a trade when the price reaches a level you decided in advance, to limit the loss.

Volatility. How much and how quickly a price moves. Crypto is known for high volatility.

Whale. A person or group holding a very large amount of a cryptocurrency, able to move its price.

From words to habits

Knowing the terms is only the start. Terms such as leverage and liquidation matter because they connect directly to risk management, which is where Dilshad's approach begins. Learn the vocabulary, then build a written process around it.
